About Us

With our wide experience of implementing MDM across many enterprises, we realised that the customers don’t want a full fledged MDM with all bells and whistles. Gartner recognises this in their Magic Quadrant report and says there is a Gap in the market for a mid-tier solution that is more than just Dedup solution but delivers most critical capabilities of an MDM. With this in mind, we have a created this solution called SINGL.

About SiNGL

SiNGL collates the master data from disparate source systems, cleanses, de-duplicates using industry specific algorithms and delivers a “Golden Record”. This Golden Record is stored in a Central Repository and can then be distributed back to the sources systems and to downstream applications–in batch and real-time.SiNGL can be deployed on-prem and any cloud.
